Orange set Orange #EC5E27 + Milk #FDFDF1

Your brand colours in four approaches.

A · Ink & Orange Recommended

White and black base. Orange only on primary buttons, Save, the active tab and the selected interest.

Why it works
  • Creators’ work is the colour on the page
  • Orange instantly means “do this”
  • Easiest to keep consistent as the product grows
Watch out
  • Brand feel is quieter; the logo and marketing pages carry more of it

B · Milk & Orange

The whole page in Milk, orange-tinted cards and orange buttons. Warm and very on-brand.

Why it works
  • Feels warm, crafted and distinctly yours
  • Milk is gentler on the eyes than pure white
Watch out
  • Orange card tints show only while images load; real work will cover them
  • Warm backgrounds slightly shift how colours in uploaded work look

C · Orange Frame

Milk content area framed by a solid orange sidebar and header. The boldest brand presence.

Why it works
  • Unmistakable in screenshots and on social media
  • The frame stays put while the work scrolls
Watch out
  • A big orange area competes with the work for attention
  • Can feel loud for long browsing sessions

D · Night Market

Warm near-black base with Milk text and orange accents. Moody and premium.

Why it works
  • Work and motion pop on a dark background
  • Orange glows; feels premium
Watch out
  • Best as an optional dark mode alongside A or B, not the only theme

Lime set Dark Grey #0E0E0B + Lime Green #AFE607

The same four approaches, using the colours from your reference. Lime is always paired with dark text, because white on lime is unreadable.

E · Ink & Lime Recommended in set

White and near-black base. Lime only on primary buttons, Save, the active tab and the selected interest.

Why it works
  • The work stays the hero; lime makes every action jump out
  • Cleanest and easiest to scale
  • Closest to your reference, but on light
Watch out
  • Lime on white has low edge contrast, so buttons rely on their dark text
  • Lime text on white is unreadable, so liked hearts use a darker olive

F · Milk & Lime

The whole page in Milk, lime-tinted cards and lime buttons. Fresh and playful.

Why it works
  • Feels fresh, youthful and very distinct
  • Milk softens the lime so it doesn’t shout
Watch out
  • Lime card tints show only while images load; real work covers them
  • A green cast shifts how colours in uploaded work look

G · Lime Frame

Light content framed by a solid lime sidebar and header, with dark buttons that have lime text. The boldest lime presence.

Why it works
  • Instantly recognisable in screenshots
  • Dark buttons with lime text feel sharp and premium
Watch out
  • A large lime area is tiring over long sessions
  • Competes with the work more than any other option

H · Night Volt

Dark Grey base with off-white text, lime actions and lime-tinted motion. Exactly your reference.

Why it works
  • Highest energy; lime on near-black has about 14:1 contrast
  • Work and motion pop on the dark base
Watch out
  • Lime and black is a common tech and crypto look
  • Best as a dark mode alongside E or F, not the only theme
